# StudentHub Portal

## Project Overview
StudentHub Portal is a responsive web-based student management system designed to provide students with a centralized platform for accessing academic information and campus services. The portal simplifies communication between students and the institution by providing quick access to attendance, courses, assignments, study materials, results, notices, library resources, placement opportunities, and more.

---

## Objectives

- Provide a single platform for academic activities.
- Improve communication between students and the institution.
- Allow students to easily access learning resources.
- Manage academic records efficiently.
- Enhance the overall student experience.
